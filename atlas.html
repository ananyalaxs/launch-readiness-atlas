Readiness score and the layers it is built from, for all 50 states and DC. Click a row to open it on the map. Select up to 3 states to compare.

Clinics

Clinic directory

Every US clinic that has run a registered trial of the drugs covered here, one row per clinic. Click a row to open the clinic's full page. The list follows the map's filters and company lens; type below to narrow it, or export it as CSV.

Each company gets one line, with the compounds it has tested at that clinic and its number of trials there. Status, companies and compounds come from ClinicalTrials.gov: a company is a lead sponsor the registry classes as industry, and a compound is the code the registry gives for the drug. Other sponsors, such as universities, are on each clinic's page.

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, data . Facts only.
